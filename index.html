                  Safety first

                  Some jobs should stop before they start.

                  A to Z Wise AI always flags high-risk situations. When you see these signs, stop troubleshooting and contact a qualified professional or emergency service.

                  ⚡Electrical hazardsSparks, shocks, burning smells, or exposed wiring.
                  🔥Fire or smokeAny sign of fire, smoke, or burning odor.
                  ☠️Gas leaks & carbon monoxideGas smell, hissing near appliances, or a CO alarm.
                  🏚️Structural damageWide or growing cracks, sagging floors or ceilings.
                  🧪Hazardous materialsSuspected asbestos, lead paint, or chemical spills.
                  💧Major water leaks & sewageFlooding, burst pipes, or sewage backup.
                  🦠Dangerous moldLarge mold areas or health symptoms from exposure.
                  🪜Roofing & fall hazardsSteep roofs, heights, or unstable ladders.

                  If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number before doing anything else.
