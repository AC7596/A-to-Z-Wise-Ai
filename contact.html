How do I access Founding Member benefits?

Your Founding Member subscription supports A to Z Wise AI and gives you access to the features currently available on the public site, plus eligibility for future member updates as new features are released.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Your $9.99/month Founding Member subscription is recurring monthly until canceled. You can cancel it through PayPal.

No. The current public site does not provide customer accounts or saved cloud history.

Is A to Z Wise AI a replacement for a contractor?

No. A to Z Wise AI helps you understand problems before calling a pro, but high-risk or complex repairs should be handled by licensed contractors.
