Safety first for every repair

⚠️ EMERGENCY WARNING

If you suspect a gas leak, carbon monoxide (CO) exposure, active fire, or structural failure, evacuate immediately and call 911 or your local emergency services.

Critical Safety Signals

⚡Electrical Shock & SparksNever touch exposed live wires. Turn off power at the main breaker before inspecting fixtures.
☠️Carbon Monoxide (CO)CO is invisible and odorless. An sounding CO alarm requires immediate evacuation to fresh air.
🔥Gas Odors (Sulfur / Rotten Eggs)Do not flip electrical switches or create sparks. Evacuate and call your gas provider.
💧Major Flooding & SewageShut off main water valve immediately. Avoid contact with contaminated waste water.

Understanding Carbon Monoxide (CO) vs Carbon Dioxide (CO2)

A to Z Wise AI distinguishes strictly between dangerous Carbon Monoxide (CO) emergencies and Carbon Dioxide (CO2) air quality issues:

  • Carbon Monoxide (CO): A lethal, colorless, odorless gas produced by fuel-burning appliances. Any CO alarm triggers an immediate STOP response.
  • Carbon Dioxide (CO2): A gas associated with room ventilation or soda equipment. Handled with lower-severity ventilation guidance.

Informational Guidance Disclaimer

A to Z Wise AI provides informational guidance only and cannot replace licensed, qualified professionals when structural, electrical, gas, or plumbing safety is at risk. Always prioritize personal safety.
