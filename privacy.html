Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

A to Z Wise AI respects your privacy. This policy describes the current data handling of the public A to Z Wise AI static website.

2. Information Stored in Your Browser

The current A to Z Wise AI website uses browser storage on your device to keep parts of the experience working locally. This includes temporary diagnosis session details in sessionStorage for the current tab and selected profile, equipment, maintenance reminder, and activity data in localStorage. This information stays in your browser and is not uploaded to A to Z Wise AI by the current static-site implementation.

3. Diagnosis Entries, Photos, and AI Processing

When you use the current public diagnosis tools, the information you type and any photos you select are used in your browser for the on-site experience. Diagnosis descriptions are sent to our Cloudflare-hosted service and its AI provider to generate a response. The site does not provide saved cloud diagnosis history or automatic AI photo analysis. A clearly labeled local Demo Mode may be used if the AI service is unavailable. If a future secure diagnosis backend is connected, selected diagnosis details and any explicitly selected My Home equipment context could be sent to that backend over HTTPS without exposing any AI provider key in the browser, and this policy should be updated before that public rollout.

4. PayPal Payments and Member Sign-in

Founding Member subscription payments are handled by PayPal. Payment card details and PayPal billing information are handled by PayPal, not stored by the current A to Z Wise AI static GitHub Pages site, and are subject to PayPal's own terms and privacy practices.

Email account sign-in and private cloud records are being prepared. When enabled, Supabase manages email verification and account sessions. An email delivery provider sends the sign-in code. You do not give us your PayPal password. To link a paid subscription, our server compares your verified account email with the email on that PayPal subscription and stores your account ID, subscription ID, and PayPal payer ID. Payment status is checked before paid AI access.

Account sessions are stored in this browser and can refresh while you remain signed in. Use Sign out on shared devices. Signing out does not remove local My Home records or downloaded backups. Service providers may retain operational logs; our application does not intentionally log authentication tokens or PayPal profile responses.

Cloud transfers are optional and manual. Choosing Save this device to cloud sends your My Home record�including any address, equipment identifiers, notes, service history, and document links you entered�to Supabase. Access is restricted to your signed-in account. Files linked in the record are not uploaded. Loading a cloud copy replaces the local record after a backup download. Cloud records remain until removed through account support; contact us for account or data deletion. Do not save sensitive information in publicly accessible document links.

5. Cookies, Analytics, and Future Features

Based on the current implementation, the public A to Z Wise AI static site does not run its own analytics platform, advertising trackers, or advertising-based profiling. Account and cloud-storage controls remain unavailable until setup and testing are complete.

6. Privacy Contact

For privacy questions about the current A to Z Wise AI site or Founding Member subscription, please use the A to Z Wise AI Contact page. Entity/service name: A to Z Wise AI. Official website: https://atozwiseai.com.
